\caption[One test for a full interval]{Fix the classical target,
  \(e_D=2\), \(e_L=0\), and \(\alpha=1/2+9\varepsilon<1\).
  Choose \((\mathfrak F,C_H)\) meeting the
  \hyperlink{proof.join-size-requirements}{joint size requirements},
  then fix its constants and threshold \(x_*\).
  The family supplies arbitrarily distant full intervals above \(x_*\).
  In a given interval the prime sums select \(x_I\) before any
  lattice is evaluated. At \(x_I\) choose the label \(u_*\) using the
  host's Fourier sum. The
  \hyperlink{proof.section-run-family-identification}{section runs}
  and \hyperlink{proof.ramified-donor-identification}{donor} supply
  the lattice at \(i(u_*)\), with the same arrays, weights, cutoff
  and centering used in Fourier inversion. By the extremal property of
  \(x_I\), the sign of the excess there gives the bound throughout \(I\).}
