# From Theta Periods to the Riemann Hypothesis

Our two goals are to reconstruct and check Levent Alpöge’s announced argument for the classical Riemann hypothesis in ordinary mathematical language and to identify its genuinely new mathematical contributions. The paper follows iterated theta lifts to a one-sided estimate for a centered prime-power sum and shows how that estimate excludes nontrivial zeta zeros off the critical line. Section 6 also gives the Mellin sign-change argument for primitive Dirichlet characters from the corresponding window estimate for each target.

## Method

To reconstruct the full argument and identify genuinely new mathematics, we begin with a coarse directed acyclic graph (DAG) of implications. AI agents follow the map to select a step, refine it as needed and write its mathematical proof. As they write, they update the map with the new substeps, their exact inputs and conclusions, their dependencies and links to the proofs. In every view, the upstream conclusions must jointly supply all the inputs to each downstream step under the stated premises, with matching witnesses, data, normalizations and quantifier order. The finer steps must recover the parent conclusion from the original hypotheses. Where a step recovers known mathematics, we cite the precise result from the primary literature in place of a repeated derivation, after checking that the application meets its hypotheses and its conclusion supplies the input needed downstream. For the remaining steps we give our own mathematical derivations and consult the pinned Lean source where helpful. We gradually reduce our reliance on Lean so that readers can ultimately check the entire argument from the exposition and its primary references alone. Alpöge’s announcement and Sahibzada Allahyar’s public reconstruction served as early orientation. A substantive new idea, range of uniformity or connection can merit a separate entry, even when established by a short deduction. Routine calculations, restatements and straightforward corollaries appear where they help explain the proof. An independent subagent checks each new or revised proof. Each suspected new result goes to an Astra reviewer in a fresh context with no project memory or prior verdict. The reviewer compares the precise statement with mathematics established before the announcement and assesses its intrinsic significance. ### Arithmetic windows and Galois cohomology

- **Uniform higher fine Selmer bounds.** Fix the target, precision $0<\varepsilon<1/52$ and dyadic profile. Choose $J$ before the auxiliary primes. For sufficiently large $X$ and each admissible pair of auxiliary primes at $Z=X^{1/J}$, one subinterval $[W,W^2]\subseteq[Z,X]$ works at every real $y$ in it. On the prescribed CM fields, the sum of the localization-kernel lengths weighted by $\log\ell$ over primes $\ell$ and twists $(-r)$ with $1\le r<\lfloor y^{401}\rfloor$ is at most $\exp(y^\varepsilon/2)$. Duality and $K$-theory give fixed-field and fixed-twist bounds. For zeta, the required twist estimates follow from published bounds and the explicit formula. The uniform Selmer bound as the fields and twists vary supplies the input for the local division step. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.family)

- **Lifting integral finite cohomology classes with separated Hodge jumps.** Let $T_1\twoheadrightarrow T_2$ be a surjection of crystalline lattices at an unramified field of degree $n$ over $\mathbf Q_\ell$, $\ell\ge11$. The local question asks how much of $H_f^1(T_2)$ lifts integrally. Along the Frobenius cycle the large signed Hodge jumps may occur singly or in pairs, with at least $j$ small labels between successive groups. For target rank $r$, height $H$ bounding the full prescribed intervals and combined small width $g$, the bound on the cokernel length, normalized by the absolute ramification index of the coefficient field, is $O((n+1)r[H/(\ell-1)^{j+1}+(g+1)(j+1)+\log_\ell\max(1,H)])$. The dependence on cyclic spacing is the substantive point. The estimate supplies one inert-place contribution to the Hecke lattice comparison. Section 4 proves the estimate by lifting classes over each group of jumps, treating both orders of opposite signs and gluing without a loss for the number of groups. [Proof of the lifting bound](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=proof.crystalline-cluster-lifting) · [Full hypotheses and application](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.crystalline-rows) · [Zhu, Theorem B](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.1078v3#page=3) · [Čoupek–Gazaki–Marmora, Corollary 3.16](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.26035v3#page=30)

- **Which torsion extensions come from fixed crystalline endpoints?** Let $K/\mathbf Q_\ell$ be unramified, $\ell\ge5$. Fix a crystalline $\mathcal O$-lattice $T$ for $G_K$ and crystalline characters $\chi_c=\chi_1\varepsilon_\ell^{1-c}$. The theorem prescribes their labelled Hodge jumps and separates the other jumps from explicit residue intervals. Let $\lambda$ be a uniformizer and $X$ a finite coefficient module. For all sufficiently large $a$ with $\lambda^aX=0$, require the extension of $(\mathcal O/\lambda^a)(\chi_1)$ by $T\otimes X$ to occur as a quotient of nested stable lattices. The ambient space is a finite direct sum of crystalline representations, each with the prescribed full jumps and residual characteristic polynomials. The classes passing this test are exactly the image of $H_f^1(K,T(\chi_1^{-1}))\otimes X$. For the higher character slots the image is zero, and for the first it is the unramified subgroup. Consequently congruent crystalline lattices of this labelled type have the same finite image modulo the congruence, even for some weight intervals wider than the Fontaine–Laffaille range. This supplies the zero local loss at the qualifying large primes. An earlier short crystalline result over $\mathbf Q_\ell$ constructs suitable endpoint lifts of residue-field extensions. [Statement and hypotheses](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.residual-crystalline-admissibility) · [Atobe et al., Lemma 7.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.07582v4#page=25)

- **Lifting integral finite classes at a prime of tame type.** For a surjection of stable lattices in potentially crystalline representations of $G_{\mathbf Q_p}$, assume that both tame inertial types use at most $N$ distinct characters in one Frobenius-stable set containing $1$. The signed filtration jumps lie in a common set of at most $N+1$ integers in $[-\eta,H]$ containing zero. Write $q$ for the largest Frobenius orbit of a ratio of the characters. For $N\ge2$, $1\le\eta<H$ and $p>40(HN^2+N^3)$, the cokernel on integral $H_f^1$ is killed by $p^{7(Nq+\eta(N+q))}$. Once the prime meets this threshold, the exponent is independent of $H$, the tame splitting degree and the multiplicities. The two type primes in the argument require only $q\le\lfloor N^2/4\rfloor$ and the resulting rank-dependent length bound. Earlier Breuil–Kisin exactness gives an unspecified annihilator for the underlying module cokernel. The uniform bound for lifting finite classes is the candidate. [Local statement and proof](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.tame-type-lifting) · [Čoupek–Gazaki–Marmora, Proposition 3.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.26035v3#page=24)

- **Integral Selmer bounds for tame anticyclotomic twists.** Fix the finite collection of odd-rank cuspidal lattices at one stage, with their polarizations, Hodge gaps and local conditions. One tame character, anticyclotomic and trivial at every place over $\ell$, must work for all lattices and every power prime to $\ell$. The sum of the excesses of their normalized Selmer lengths above $\nu_\ell$ and the total tame inertia cost can both be made arbitrarily small. Here $\nu_\ell=0$ at odd primes and $\nu_2=1$ on the classical family. Section 4 obtains the required bounded-degree coefficients in the integral Selmer determinants on the same split prime pairs for the prescribed Galois representations. Simultaneous specialization gives the common character. Section 4 identifies the common phase of the marked theta evaluations on all auxiliary covers and realizes the specialized columns in the prescribed twisted lattice. Its congruence index also uses estimates for local Galois cohomology. [Full statement and derivation](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.tame-selmer) · [Mazur–Rubin, Proposition 9.17 and Remark 10.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.04642v3) · [Smith, Theorem 2.14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.05143v2#page=10)

### Theta and Bessel periods

- **Comparison of the prescribed tests across words.** At one selected translate, one finite adelic central element and one row isometry serve all lift words. Each word has a unitary column change and a nonzero scalar that work for every twisting tuple on the ramified columns and projected top rows. A positive power of the relative normalization ratio is rational and supported on the tuple’s twisting primes. Seesaw identities compare matched kernels, and the squared Bessel period formula applies to a fixed everywhere-tempered cuspidal representation with discrete Hermitian weak base change. The simultaneous comparison gives rank transfer through one row dual over the coefficient field and preserves valuations at the places avoided by the twists. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.periods) · [Fixed-representation period formula](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2025.8)

- **Complementary-frame transfer at a split place.** A prescribed integral test on one path around a theta-grid square gives a test on the other path by partial Fourier transforms and integration over complementary primitive frames. The transferred test has the required lattice support, congruence depth and level-character conditions, and its normalized local zeta section agrees with the complementary section with its determinant twist. Seesaw supplies the principle of comparing matched kernels. This local identification gives the matching needed for changing paths in the integral row tests. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.transfer)

- **Detecting a smaller-group cusp form at the prescribed principal level.** Fix the precision and depth constant. Beyond one threshold for the admissible grids, let $Y$ be the bottom Hermitian space and $V=Y\perp\langle1\rangle$. Every nonzero cuspidal multiplicity map $t$ on $U(Y)$ of the prescribed real type at principal level $(g_0g_1)$ and every nonzero smooth real vector $w$ admits a test cusp form $a$ on $U(V)$ with a nonzero bilinear period against $t(w)$. The larger real type is specified, the testing vector is $K_\infty$-finite, and $a$ must have fixed vectors for the one principal level prescribed before $t,w$ vary. Both level characters are trivial. The substantive point is the uniform depth when the fields, ranks and weights vary together. Section 2 gives the proof on the prescribed grids. At a point where the row is large, it bounds the other terms of a Poincaré period by less than the stabilizer contribution. Compact averaging gives a test vector of the prescribed kind. The paper then proves that the descending lifts span the core tests and that the pairing survives ascent on the core tower. [Statement and argument](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.fixed-level-period) · [Global GGP comparison](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.1007/s10240-021-00129-1/)

### Relative traces and automorphic multiplicities

Fix the selected family. At each selected translate the normalized Bessel Poincaré operators $L_w$ on the full columns have one Petersson target. Write $d_w$ for the full column dimension, $D_\star$ for the reference row dimension and $b=\exp(x^\varepsilon)$.

- **Mixed Gram estimate.** Beyond a common threshold the bound $\lVert L_w^*L_v\rVert_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\le(1+b^{-6})d_wd_v/D_\star$ holds for every distinct pair at every selected translate. Relative trace unfolding reindexes the terms by orbits, and local densities give the primitive counts. The uniform estimate uses the signed scalar main term and the signed sum over corank-one pairs failing local generation. It controls the mixed terms in the rank calculation. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.trace)

- **Same-word Gram defect.** For every word the mean-square defect $\lVert L_w^*L_w-I\rVert_{\mathrm{HS}}^2/d_w$ is at most $e_0$, with $\sqrt{e_0}\le\exp(-x^{\varepsilon/8})$ beyond a common threshold. Schur orthogonality supplies the identity term, and the other degenerate and nondegenerate orbits require uniform bounds. This controls the kernel and, with the ramified-column dimension, gives a nonzero reference pairing. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.diagonal)

- **Uniform automorphic multiplicity count.** For the specified finite projector $e$ and discrete-series weights on $U(p,q)$ with $q^2\le r=p+q$, the reference count is the product of formal degrees, the adelic quotient volume and $\Re e(1)$. The relative error is at most $\exp(-rf\log r/16)$ as the unitary rank $r$ and the totally real base-field degree $f$ vary, within the prescribed level and weight-gap range. Known fixed-group estimates give precedents. The full-space count feeds the trace moments. The subsequent distinguished-row proportion uses counts with compatible projectors and the corresponding local masses. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.count)

### Integral lattices and divisibility

- **A short window for a perturbed determinant jet.** For a prime $p\ge5$, odd Frobenius distance $h$, matrix size $r$ and the stated perturbation bounds, every interval of $\lceil64r^2(h+3+3\log_p(r+1))\rceil$ consecutive nonnegative orders contains a jet whose valuation is at most zero after normalization by the specified power of $p$ and Cayley factorial. Cayley’s identity gives the factorial normalization, and finite differences use the bound on the perturbed Newton coefficients. The resulting short window gives an available integral-germ test for an odd Frobenius block. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.jet)

- **Realizing Frobenius gains by division.** At a sufficiently large scale with the prescribed Artin data, weights and period maps, lower valuation bounds for the period values on the actual integral tests allow division by fractional ideals. The gap between the inertia-weighted ramp sum and the gains retained after restriction, and the allowance for lattice indices, satisfy the same uniform loss bound. Height, slope and CM-period comparisons then give the centered inequality $D(u)\le E_x$ at every tied translate with cutoff $N=\lceil2a_E\rceil$. This is the input to Fourier inversion at the same scale. In §6 one family supplies the hypotheses throughout full intervals for use in the published Mellin sign-change argument. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.gain)

- **Integral Weyl lattices at a single weight and prime.** Let $\lambda=(a^t,\kappa)$ be a dominant polynomial weight and use the same primitive highest vector for the Weyl lattice $\Delta$ and its contravariant dual $\nabla$. At grade $i$ form the monic least common multiple $D_i(T)$ of the content polynomials of the allowed $i$-box skew shapes that survive at $T=a$. If $p^{e_L}$ clears the second-block lattices up to a given depth, then $p^{e_L+v_p(D_i(a))}\nabla(\lambda)_v\subseteq\Delta(\lambda)_v$ at each weight of that grade within the same cutoff. The bound holds at singular specializations and at primes dividing $i!$. Even in a two-dimensional weight space, two distinct factors can each contribute a power of the same prime. Nakahama gives the rational scalars. The candidate concerns their denominators on the integral lattice. The weaker bound summed over primes that classical RH uses follows from the rational formula with ordinary integral bounds for its projectors. Historical priority for the sharper statement is open. [Precise weightwise bound and proof](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.integral-weyl-content) · [Nakahama, Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.05919#page=5)

- **Determinant divisibility in a formal group of mixed signature.** Let $p$ be odd. Over the valuation ring of an algebraically closed valued field of characteristic zero, let a commutative formal group have logarithmic characters $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{p^s}$ for the action of $\mu_{p^h-1}$, with $h>0$ even and each odd type of dimension at most $j$. Choose any integral rank-$i$ frame with a unit minor in one even type. For each $c\ge1$ the $c$-th power of the determinant of the invariant derivatives, one column per copy of the group, takes every integral germ at the origin to a value of valuation at least $(i-r)cp/(p^2-1)-i^2(1+\log_p c)$, where $0\le r\le\min(i,j(3+\log_p c)/2)$ and $v(p)=1$. The scalar height-two coefficient is classical. The candidate controls the number of directions that can lose that gain in an arbitrary mixed-type frame. Retaining a separate radius for each successive Frobenius stage gives the stronger lower bound $icp/(p^2-1)-jcp^3/(p^2-1)^2-i^2\log_p(c+1)$. The paper derives this refinement from the stage construction. The local height estimate at the small inert primes already follows from the original bound. Historical priority for the mixed-type bound is open. [Local statement and proof](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.mixed-determinant-depth) · [Katz, §V](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_1977__41-42__55_0.pdf#page=9) · [Schneider–Teitelbaum, Appendix (c)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0102012#page=33)

- **A Frobenius pairing on two prescribed tangent planes.** Take a prime $p\ge5$ in the alternating CM-type cases of Section 4. The polarized semiabelian formal group has alternating tangent ranks $n+j$ and $j'$. Fix two integral rank-$n$ direct summands in the paired large types $a$ and $\overline{a+i}$, for one odd $0<i<D$ in the full Frobenius cycle of length $D$. On saturated subplanes of equal rank at least $\max(0,n-ij'-j)$, the polarized biextension singles out a mixed Taylor coefficient. In formal logarithm coordinates, its term of bidegree $(q,1)$, where $q=p^i$, becomes a perfect Frobenius pairing over the residue field after multiplication by $p^{1+(q-p)/(p^2-1)}$. The proof also bounds the remaining coefficients on these subplanes. This supplies an input to the determinant estimate and determines the valuation of individual mixed derivatives before any determinant is taken. Zink constructs the underlying biextension from a pairing of displays. The prescribed planes and the uniform rank and coefficient estimates are the subject of the candidate. Historical priority remains open. [Local statement and proof](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.marked-frobenius-pairing) · [Zink, §4.3](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_2002__278__127_0.pdf#page=113)

### Heights, section lattices and Fourier–Jacobi coefficients

- **Finite-degree bound for an adjoint section lattice.** On a nonterminal cover component $Z$, the lower bound for the degree of the actual adjoint section lattice in the prescribed metrics has leading Chow-height term $Q\ell h_Z$ at the specified degree $\ell$. David–Philippon and Chen give earlier finite-degree determinant estimates using the Chow form. The narrower comparison concerns this selected degree and rank, with uniform control of the norm and index losses in transferring to the actual lattice. Descent and truncation turn the component bounds into covolume estimates for cusp sections in the indefinite tower. These enter the division estimates. A separate calculation gives the reference slopes of the definite column lattices. [Precise statement](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.height)

- **Arithmetic height of the full flag at a finite cutoff.** On a nonterminal unitary cover of constant signature $(p,q)$ over the prescribed abelian CM field $E$ of degree $2f$, take $f\ge2$, $q\ge1$ and $N=p+q\ge3q+1$. Use the prescribed good level and vary the integral flags at the exceptional primes and the primes up to $B$. Each of the last $q$ weights at a label must be separated from every other weight there by at least $g$. The cutoff satisfies

  ```math
  B+1 > 4fN^2 \left(1+\frac{\lVert u\rVert_{\infty}}{g}\right).
  ```

  The component-volume-weighted mean height $H_B(u)$ satisfies

  ```math
  \begin{aligned}
  &H_B(u) \ge -C\Bigl(\Lambda_B \lVert u\rVert_1\\
  &\qquad {}+ q\sum_{\tau}\bigl(\max_i u_{\tau i}-\min_i u_{\tau i}\bigr)\Bigr),\\[3pt]
  &\Lambda_B = \log\!\bigl(B\,\operatorname{rd}(E)\bigr)\\
  &\qquad {}+ \left\lvert\Re\!\left(\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi_{d_0})\right)\right\rvert .
  \end{aligned}
  ```

  This supplies a geometric input to the finite-degree estimate for sections. The paper computes the change on the common finite model when a good prime is added, including its toroidal boundary, and bounds the resulting prime tail. On these same components it compares mixed intersections for the limiting Hodge metric. Testing weights on the walls of the dominant chamber and calibrating the two surviving determinant terms gives the bound at the original cutoff. The application to the section lattice also uses a finite-place trace and section norms. Historical priority is open. [Statement and mathematical status](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.flag-height)

- **Detecting a prescribed low Fourier–Jacobi coefficient at a CM boundary.** Fix a rank-one cusp direction on either nonterminal principal-level unitary cover and a positive frequency of trace at most the cutoff. If the coefficient of a cusp section there is nonzero, a one-parameter degeneration with CM abelian part and torsion extension data detects it. One Laurent coefficient and one monomial in the tangent-frame variables define a linear test that kills every section with zero coefficient at the specified frequency and direction. The torsion denominator and the lengths of the CM tangent generators are controlled uniformly as the field, rank, level, cutoff and weight vary. The same test controls finite denominators and complex norms. This gives a lower bound for the first minimum after quotienting by the sections with all low coefficients zero. Earlier work uses Heegner points to estimate integral differentials on Shimura curves and torsion specializations to test Siegel Jacobi forms. [Statement, sizes and proof](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.cm-boundary-fourier-jacobi) · [Pasten, §§13–14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.09251v4#page=58) · [Richter–Westerholt-Raum, proof of Proposition 2.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.07733v1#page=5)

为重构整套论证并找出其中真正新增的数学，我们先在大颗粒度上用有向无环图（DAG）画出主要推导。AI 智能体依图选取一个步骤，按需要细化并写出数学证明，同时在图上补入新增的子步骤、确切的输入和结论、依赖关系及正文链接。无论图展开到哪一层，在所列前提下，上游结论都须同时给出下游每一步的全部输入。各路推导汇合时，所用的族、选定的对象和数据须相同，归一化与量词顺序也须相符。细化后的各步须在原假设下推出原结论。遇到已有的结果，先在原始文献中定位确切的定理或论证。核对当前对象满足其假设、其结论足以提供下游所需的输入后，就引用该结果，省去重复的推导。其余步骤由我们给出数学推导，必要时查阅固定版本的 Lean 源码。我们逐步减少对 Lean 的依赖，使读者最终只凭正文中的数学推导和所引的权威文献就能核对整套证明。Alpöge 的公告和 Sahibzada Allahyar 的公开重构稿用于初读。能提出有分量的新思路、扩大一致性结论的范围或建立新的数学联系，即使推导简短，也值得单列为候选。例行计算、改写和常规推论放在相应的证明中。每项新增或修订的证明都由一个独立子智能体核查。每项疑似新成果另请 Astra 在不带先前评议和项目记忆的新上下文中复核。它从精确命题出发，与公告前数学家已经建立的结果比较，并评估命题本身的数学价值。 [Alpöge 的公告](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf) · [Allahyar 的公开重构稿](https://github.com/sahibzada-allahyar/math/blob/rh-exposition-2026-11-20-r2/preprint/alpoge-riemann/reconstruction.pdf) · [固定版本的 Lean 源码](https://github.com/TomasOrtega/RH/tree/a6ac91a412dcbaab31801355ec5707024a71bf65/extracted/lean)

### 算术区间与 Galois 上同调

- **高阶细 Selmer 群的一致界。** 固定目标、精度 $0<\varepsilon<1/52$ 及二次幂层的选择规则，再于选取辅助素数前确定 $J$。$X$ 充分大时，在 $Z=X^{1/J}$ 处给定任一对符合要求的辅助素数，都有一个子区间 $[W,W^2]\subseteq[Z,X]$，其中每个实数 $y$ 都适用同一结论。在指定的 CM 域上，局部化核的长度按素数 $\ell$ 以 $\log\ell$ 加权，再对素数和满足 $1\le r<\lfloor y^{401}\rfloor$ 的 Tate 扭曲 $(-r)$ 求和，总和至多为 $\exp(y^\varepsilon/2)$。对偶与 $K$ 理论给出固定数域和固定扭曲下的界。经典 ζ 情形所需的扭曲估计可由已发表的界及显式公式推出。数域和扭曲变化时的一致 Selmer 界为局部除法提供输入。 [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.family)

- **Hodge 跳跃分隔时整有限上同调类的提升。** 设 $K/\mathbf Q_\ell$ 为 $n$ 次非分歧扩张，$\ell\ge11$，给定晶体表示的稳定格满射 $T_1\twoheadrightarrow T_2$。问题是 $H_f^1(K,T_2)$ 中有多少类能整地提升。沿 Frobenius 的循环顺序，大的带符号 Hodge 跳跃可单独或成对出现，相邻两组之间至少有 $j$ 个小指标。设目标格的秩为 $r$，所有指定区间端点的绝对值上界为 $H$，三个小宽度参数之和为 $g$。余核长度除以系数域的绝对分歧指数后，至多为 $O((n+1)r[H/(\ell-1)^{j+1}+(g+1)(j+1)+\log_\ell\max(1,H)])$。值得单列的是，指标在 Frobenius 循环上的间隔能压低大跳跃的代价。第 4 节给出这项惰性位估计的证明。论证逐组提升上同调类，分别处理正、负跳跃的两种次序，再拼合各组的提升，所得界不随组数累加。[提升估计的证明](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=proof.crystalline-cluster-lifting) · [完整假设与用法](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.crystalline-rows) · [Zhu 的定理 B](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.1078v3#page=3) · [Čoupek–Gazaki–Marmora 的推论 3.16](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.26035v3#page=30)

- **哪些挠扩张来自指定的晶体表示格？** 设 $K/\mathbf Q_\ell$ 为非分歧扩张，$\ell\ge5$。固定 $G_K$ 的晶体表示及其稳定 $\mathcal O$-格 $T$，并固定一列晶体特征标 $\chi_c=\chi_1\varepsilon_\ell^{1-c}$。定理对每个嵌入的 Hodge 跳跃及其模 $\ell$ 的剩余类给出明确的分离条件。取 $\mathcal O$ 的素元 $\lambda$ 和有限 $\mathcal O$-模 $X$。一个上同调类给出短正合列 $0\to T\otimes X\to E_a\to(\mathcal O/\lambda^a)(\chi_1)\to0$。要求对一切充分大且满足 $\lambda^a X=0$ 的 $a$，中间项 $E_a$ 都能写成两个相嵌的稳定格之商。这些格位于晶体表示的直和里，每个直和项均须具有指定的全部跳跃和剩余特征多项式。满足条件的扩张类恰为 $H_f^1(K,T(\chi_1^{-1}))\otimes X$ 的像。把 $T$ 换成较高的特征标时，所得群为零。对首个特征标，所得群是非分歧子群。因此，同一 Hodge 型的两个晶体格若模某个素元幂同余，它们的有限子群在该精度下也有相同的像。结论覆盖某些超出 Fontaine–Laffaille 范围的权，并使符合条件的大素数处的局部损失为零。此前 Atobe 等人在 $\mathbf Q_\ell$ 上的短晶体范围内，为剩余域上的扩张选取了适当的两端提升。[精确条件和命题](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.residual-crystalline-admissibility) · [Atobe 等，引理 7.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.07582v4#page=25)

- **温和惯性型下整有限上同调类的提升。** 给定 $G_{\mathbf Q_p}$ 的潜在晶体表示之间的稳定格满射。设两端的惯性型都取自同一个 Frobenius 稳定的温和特征标集合，该集合含平凡特征标，大小至多为 $N$。两端的滤过跳跃均取自 $[-\eta,H]$ 中同一个含零的整数集，其大小至多为 $N+1$。设 $q$ 是任意两个特征标之商的 Frobenius 轨道长度的最大值。若 $N\ge2$、$1\le\eta<H$ 且 $p>40(HN^2+N^3)$，则 $p^{7(Nq+\eta(N+q))}$ 消去整 $H_f^1$ 映射的余核。一旦素数达到阈值，这一指数与正端点 $H$、温和分裂域的次数以及特征标和跳跃的重数无关。论证在指定惯性型的两个素数处只用到 $q\le\lfloor N^2/4\rfloor$ 及由此得到的依赖目标秩的长度界。已有的 Breuil–Kisin 函子正合性结果给出底层模余核的某个零化子。这里单列的候选是对有限上同调类整提升的一致定量控制。[局部命题与证明](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.tame-type-lifting) · [Čoupek–Gazaki–Marmora，命题 3.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.26035v3#page=24)

- **温和分歧的反循环扭曲下，整 Selmer 群的长度上界。** 固定某一阶段的有限组奇数秩尖点表示格，以及它们的极化、Hodge 跳跃间隙和局部条件。要找到一个温和分歧的反循环特征标，使它在 $\ell$ 上方每个分解群上平凡，并使同一个特征标对这组格及所有素于 $\ell$ 的幂次都适用。各个 Selmer 群归一化长度超出 $\nu_\ell$ 的部分之和，以及所有分歧点的惯性代价，都可以任意小。在经典 ζ 所用的数域上，奇素数处 $\nu_\ell=0$，而 $\nu_2=1$。第 4 节对满足所列假设的 Galois 表示，在同一批成对的分裂素点上取得每个整 Selmer 行列式所需的低赋值有界次数系数，同时特化便给出共同特征标。第 4 节比较了各辅助覆盖上带标记的 theta 取值，得到同一个归一化相位，并用指定扭曲格的整向量实现了特化后的列。这个格的同余指数还用到局部 Galois 上同调的估计。[完整命题与推导](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.tame-selmer) · [Mazur–Rubin，命题 9.17 与注 10.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.04642v3) · [Smith，定理 2.14](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.05143v2#page=10)

### Theta 与 Bessel 周期

- **不同提升序列的指定测试数据比较。** 在同一个选定平移处，对所有提升序列可用同一个有限阿代尔中心元和同一个行空间等距映射。每个序列有各自的酉列变换和非零标量，对所有扭曲元组、分歧列与投影后的顶层行都适用。相对归一化比值的某个正整数幂为有理数，其非零素赋值只出现在该元组的扭曲素数处。Seesaw 恒等式比较匹配的核，Bessel 周期模平方公式适用于固定的处处柔曼尖点表示，条件是它具有离散 Hermitian 弱基变换。上述同时比较可在系数域上通过同一个行对偶转移秩，并在扭曲素数避开的有限位保持赋值。[固定表示的周期公式](https://doi.org/10.1017/fmp.2025.8) [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.periods)

- **分裂位的互补标架转移。** 在 theta 提升网格的一个方格中，给定一条路径上的整测试函数，通过局部部分 Fourier 变换及对互补本原标架积分，构造另一条路径上的测试函数。它满足所需的格上支撑、同余深度、水平和特征标条件，其归一化局部 zeta 截面等于经行列式扭曲的互补小截面。Seesaw 提供比较匹配核的原理。这里的局部识别给出整行测试改变路径所需的具体匹配。 [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.transfer)

- **在预定的主同余水平上检测较小酉群的尖点形式。** 固定精度和深度常数。对超过同一阈值的所有容许网格，设 $Y$ 为底层 Hermitian 空间，$V=Y\perp\langle1\rangle$。在 $U(Y)$ 上，任取指定实型、主同余水平 $(g_0g_1)$ 的非零尖点重数映射 $t$，以及该实表示空间的任一非零光滑向量 $w$，都能在 $U(V)$ 上找到尖点形式 $a$，使它与 $t(w)$ 的双线性周期非零。较大群的实型已经指定，测试向量须为 $K_\infty$-有限，且 $a$ 在选取 $t,w$ 之前确定的主同余子群下不变。两边的水平特征标均平凡。这里值得单列的是数域、秩与权同时变化时，所需的水平深度仍能一致控制。第 2 节给出适用于指定网格的证明。在底层形式取值较大的一点，估计表明 Poincaré 周期中其余项之和的绝对值严格小于稳定子项的绝对值。再对极大紧子群作平均，就得到所需的测试向量。正文随后证明下降提升张成核心测试空间，并把非零配对沿核心塔传递。[命题与推导](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.fixed-level-period) · [与整体 GGP 定理的比较](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.1007/s10240-021-00129-1/)

### 相对迹与自守重数

固定所选族。在每个选定平移处，定义在完整列空间上的归一化 Bessel–Poincaré 算子 $L_w$ 有同一个 Petersson 目标空间。记 $d_w$ 为完整列空间的维数，$D_\star$ 为参考行维数，且 $b=\exp(x^\varepsilon)$。

- **不同提升序列之间的 Gram 估计。** 过了同一个阈值，任一选定平移处的不同序列都满足 $\lVert L_w^*L_v\rVert_{\mathrm{HS}}^2\le(1+b^{-6})d_wd_v/D_\star$。相对迹展开按轨道重排，局部密度给出本原计数。一致估计用到带符号的标量主项，以及未满足局部生成条件的余秩一配对的带符号和。它为秩估计控制混合项。 [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.trace)

- **同一提升序列的 Gram 偏差。** 过了同一个阈值，每个序列的均方偏差 $\lVert L_w^*L_w-I\rVert_{\mathrm{HS}}^2/d_w$ 都至多为 $e_0$，其中 $\sqrt{e_0}\le\exp(-x^{\varepsilon/8})$。Schur 正交关系给出恒等项，其余退化与非退化轨道需要一致估计。所得界控制核的维数，与分歧列空间的维数合用可给出非零的参考配对。 [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.diagonal)

- **自守重数的一致计数。** 对 $U(p,q)$ 上指定的有限位投影 $e$ 和离散系列权，设 $q^2\le r=p+q$。参考计数是形式度、阿代尔商的体积与 $\Re e(1)$ 的乘积。在所规定的水平和权间距范围内，酉群的秩 $r$ 和全实基域的次数 $f$ 可同时变化，相对误差至多为 $\exp(-rf\log r/16)$。固定群的已有估计提供了先例。完整空间的计数进入迹矩估计，后续指定行所占的比例还用到相配投影的计数及相应局部质量。 [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.count)

### 整格与整除性

- **扰动行列式射流的短区间。** 对素数 $p\ge5$、奇 Frobenius 距离 $h$、矩阵维数 $r$ 以及满足所列系数界的扰动，每段长为 $\lceil64r^2(h+3+3\log_p(r+1))\rceil$ 的连续非负整数阶中，都有一个射流在用规定的 $p$ 的幂和 Cayley 阶乘归一后，赋值至多为零。Cayley 恒等式给出阶乘归一化，有限差分法用到扰动后的 Newton 系数界。这个短区间为奇 Frobenius 块提供一个可用的整芽测试。 [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.jet)

- **通过除法实现 Frobenius 增益。** 在充分大的尺度上，使用指定的 Artin 数据、权重和周期映射，在实际整测试格上给出周期值的赋值下界，从而可以按分式理想作除法。按惯性群加权的分段线性项之和与限制后保留的增益之差，以及格指数所致的损失，都满足同一个一致误差界。结合高度、斜率和 CM 周期的比较，可在共用截断 $N=\lceil2a_E\rceil$ 下对每个相配平移推出中心化不等式 $D(u)\le E_x$。这是同一尺度上 Fourier 反演的输入。第 6 节选取同一个族，使所需假设在整个区间上成立，再用于已发表的 Mellin 变号论证。 [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.gain)

- **单个素数、单个权空间上的整 Weyl 格。** 设 $\lambda=(a^t,\kappa)$ 是占优多项式权，Weyl 格 $\Delta$ 和逆变对偶格 $\nabla$ 采用同一个本原最高权向量。对第 $i$ 层，取所有在 $T=a$ 处仍有贡献的允许斜形，每个斜形含 $i$ 个方格。设其内容多项式的首一最小公倍式为 $D_i(T)$。若 $p^{e_L}$ 能清除第二块在给定深度以内的格商，则在该层同一深度截断内的每个权 $v$ 上，有 $p^{e_L+v_p(D_i(a))}\nabla(\lambda)_v\subseteq\Delta(\lambda)_v$。奇异特化和整除 $i!$ 的素数也适用。即使权空间只有二维，两个不同的多项式因子也可能各贡献一次同一素数。Nakahama 已给出各等型分量上范数之比的公式。这里的候选是对整格分母的逐权控制。经典 RH 论证仅需对素数求和的较弱界，由该有理公式和投影算子的常规整分母估计即可得到。较强结论的文献优先性尚待考证。[逐权命题及证明](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.integral-weyl-content) · [Nakahama，定理 1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.05919#page=5)

- **带奇偶分型的形式群上不变微分算子行列式的整除性。** 设 $p$ 为奇素数，在特征零代数闭赋值域的赋值环上取一个交换形式群。设它带有 $\mu_{p^h-1}$ 的作用，$h>0$ 为偶数。在对数坐标中，第 $s$ 型上的特征标为 $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{p^s}$，其中 $s\bmod h$。各奇数型的切空间维数至多为 $j$。在任一偶数型中选取 $i$ 个整切向量，要求它们有一个 $i$ 阶子式是单位。在形式群的 $i$ 个副本上，把沿这些向量的不变微分算子排成矩阵，每个副本对应一列。对每个 $c\ge1$，其行列式的 $c$ 次幂作用于任一整函数芽，在原点得到的值满足赋值下界 $(i-r)cp/(p^2-1)-i^2(1+\log_p c)$。这里 $0\le r\le\min(i,j(3+\log_p c)/2)$，且 $v(p)=1$。一维高度二形式群的系数 $p/(p^2-1)$ 已有经典结果。候选的价值在于，对所有这样的混合型标架，一致控制可能损失这部分整除性的方向数。在各个 Frobenius 步骤中分别保留相应的半径，正文还从原构造推出较强的下界 $icp/(p^2-1)-jcp^3/(p^2-1)^2-i^2\log_p(c+1)$。RH 主线在较小惰性素数处的局部高度估计用原来的界已足够。该混合型结论的文献优先性尚待考证。[局部命题及证明](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.mixed-determinant-depth) · [Katz，第五节](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_1977__41-42__55_0.pdf#page=9) · [Schneider–Teitelbaum，附录 (c)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0102012#page=33)

- **两个给定切平面上的 Frobenius 配对。** 取第 4 节中 CM 型交替出现的素数 $p\ge5$。相应的极化半阿贝尔形式群在这些型上的切空间维数交替为 $n+j$ 和 $j'$。在长为 $D$ 的 Frobenius 轨道上固定一个奇数 $0<i<D$，并在相配的两个大维数型 $a$ 与 $\overline{a+i}$ 中各给定一个秩 $n$ 的整直和因子。从两个给定平面中可以各选一个饱和子平面，两者同秩，且秩至少为 $\max(0,n-ij'-j)$。在形式群对数坐标中，令 $q=p^i$，将极化双扩张的 $(q,1)$ 双齐次项乘以 $p^{1+(q-p)/(p^2-1)}$，约化后便得到完美的 Frobenius 配对。证明还控制了这些子平面上的其余系数。这一结果给出了行列式估计的一个输入，在取行列式之前也给出了单个混合导数的准确赋值。Zink 已从 display 的配对构造出底层双扩张。这里列为候选的是对给定切平面的秩下界与一致系数估计，首创性尚待考证。[局部命题及证明](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.marked-frobenius-pairing) · [Zink，第 4.3 节](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_2002__278__127_0.pdf#page=113)

### 高度、截面格与 Fourier–Jacobi 系数

- **伴随线丛截面格的有限次数界。** 在覆盖的非终端分量 $Z$ 上，取指定的次数 $\ell$ 和度量，实际伴随线丛截面格的度下界以 Chow 高度项 $Q\ell h_Z$ 为主项。David–Philippon 与 Chen 已用 Chow 形给出有限次数的行列式估计。这里较窄的比较要求在选定次数与秩下，一致控制转到实际格时的范数和指数损失。经下降和截断后，各分量上的界给出不定酉群塔中尖点截面格的余体积估计，供除法估计使用。定号酉群一侧列格的参考斜率另行计算。 [精确陈述](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.height)

- **有限截断下全旗簇的算术高度。** 在指定的 $2f$ 次阿贝尔 CM 数域 $E$ 上，取各无穷位符号均为 $(p,q)$ 的非终端酉群覆盖，要求 $f\ge2$、$q\ge1$、$N=p+q\ge3q+1$。固定所需的好水平，在例外素数与不超过 $B$ 的素数处更换整旗。每一标号下最后 $q$ 个权中的任一个与该标号下其余每个权的间距至少为 $g$。截断值满足

  ```math
  B+1 > 4fN^2 \left(1+\frac{\lVert u\rVert_{\infty}}{g}\right).
  ```

  以各分量的几何体积加权得到的平均高度 $H_B(u)$ 满足

  ```math
  \begin{aligned}
  &H_B(u) \ge -C\Bigl(\Lambda_B \lVert u\rVert_1\\
  &\qquad {}+ q\sum_{\tau}\bigl(\max_i u_{\tau i}-\min_i u_{\tau i}\bigr)\Bigr),\\[3pt]
  &\Lambda_B = \log\!\bigl(B\,\operatorname{rd}(E)\bigr)\\
  &\qquad {}+ \left\lvert\Re\!\left(\frac{L'}{L}(1,\chi_{d_0})\right)\right\rvert .
  \end{aligned}
  ```

  这给出了有限次数截面估计需要的一项几何输入。正文已算出加入一个好素数给共同有限整模型的交数带来的改变，涵盖环面边界，并由此控制了其余素数的可求和尾项。正文在同一批分量上比较了极限 Hodge 度量的混合交数，再由优势室壁上的权和两项行列式高度得到原截断处的下界。用于截面格时还要计入有限处的迹和截面范数。首创性尚待考证。[完整命题与证明进展](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.flag-height)

- **在 CM 边界检测一个指定的低阶 Fourier–Jacobi 系数。** 在非终端阶段的任一主同余水平酉群覆盖上，固定一个秩一尖点方向和一个迹不超过截断值的正频率。若尖点截面在这里的系数非零，就能选取一个单参数退化，使其阿贝尔部分有复乘、扩张数据为挠元，并从中读出这个系数的非零性。具体做法是取一个 Laurent 系数，再取切标架变量的一个单项式系数。这样得到的线性泛函在所有指定系数为零的截面上消失。即使数域、秩、水平、截断和权同时变化，仍可一致控制挠元的分母与 CM 切向生成元的长度。用同一泛函估计有限处的分母和无穷处的范数，可给出商格的第一极小值下界，其中取商时除去所有低阶系数均为零的截面。已有的相关工作包括以 Heegner 点估计志村曲线上的整微分，以及用挠点特化检测 Siegel 理论中的 Jacobi 形式。[精确陈述、参数范围及证明](https://adjoint-functor.github.io/theta-periods-to-rh-reader/reading-guide.pdf#nameddest=candidate.cm-boundary-fourier-jacobi) · [Pasten，第 13–14 节](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.09251v4#page=58) · [Richter 与 Westerholt-Raum，命题 2.4 的证明](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.07733v1#page=5)
